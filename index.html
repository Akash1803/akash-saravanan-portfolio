About

From ground surveys to web maps

I came to maps from the ground up — literally. I started as a civil engineer supervising construction sites, moved into GIS analysis, and spent four years turning survey data and drone mapping into answers: where to build an industrial park, how much coal sits in a stockpile, which land parcels don't match their tax records.

These days I also build the software side — web-based GIS platforms with React and Leaflet on the front and Python behind them, plus PyQGIS automation that removes the manual grind from spatial QC. I like the whole pipeline: from raw survey data to a map someone can actually make a decision with.
